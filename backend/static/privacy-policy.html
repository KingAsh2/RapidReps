Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-27 · Effective immediately

RapidReps ("we", "us") connects trainees with personal trainers. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, how it's secured, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Account info you provide directly: name, email, phone, profile photo, role.
  • Trainer info for verification: certifications, gyms worked at, ID document, payout banking info.
  • Booking & session info: scheduling, ratings, messages, payments.
  • Location: with your permission, your coarse GPS for proximity-based trainer matching and en-route session tracking.
  • Device info: push token (for notifications), OS version, app version, anonymized crash logs (Sentry).

2. How we use it

  • Match trainees with nearby trainers
  • Process payments via Stripe
  • Send transactional + marketing notifications you opted into
  • Detect and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax reporting, dispute resolution)

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only with:

  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • Expo / Apple APNs / Google FCM (push notifications)
  • Google Maps (map rendering)
  • Sentry (crash reporting — anonymized)
  • Law enforcement only when legally compelled

4. Instagram Integration META REVIEWER

Linking your Instagram account is optional. When you link:

  • We request only the instagram_business_basic permission.
  • We read your most recent 8 media items (photos, videos, reels, carousels).
  • We display only the items you explicitly select on your public RapidReps profile.
  • Your Instagram access token is AES-GCM encrypted at rest in our database — we never store it in plaintext.
  • We do not post on your behalf, read DMs, read comments, or access any other Instagram data.
  • You can unlink at any time from your RapidReps Profile screen. Unlinking immediately deletes the link record and all cached media references.
  • You can also revoke our access directly from Instagram → Settings → Apps and Websites. Meta will notify us via webhook and we'll auto-delete your link.
  • You can request full data deletion at any time by emailing privacy@rapidreps.app or via Instagram's data-deletion flow.

5. Security

  • All traffic is TLS 1.3 in transit.
  • Sensitive credentials (payment tokens, IG access tokens) are encrypted at rest.
  • Production databases are isolated and access-controlled.
  • Periodic third-party penetration tests.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correct, export, or delete your data — contact us at privacy@rapidreps.app.
  • Opt out of marketing notifications in Profile → Notifications.
  • Withdraw social-account connections at any time (in-app or via the platform).
  • CCPA / GDPR rights respected — written response within 30 days.

7. Retention

Account data: retained while your account is active + 90 days after deletion (for fraud and dispute resolution), then permanently removed. Instagram data: deleted immediately on unlink.

8. Children

RapidReps is for users 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We'll notify you in-app at least 14 days before any material change to this policy.
