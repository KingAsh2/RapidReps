## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Open the RapidReps app. On the Welcome screen you will see two options:
   - **Find a Trainer** — for trainees looking for fitness training
   - **Become a Trainer** — for certified trainers offering their services

2. Tap your choice and then tap **Sign Up**.

3. Fill in:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 6 characters)
   - Select your role: **Trainee** or **Trainer**

4. You will be guided through the onboarding flow for your role.

### Logging In

1. Tap **Log In** on the welcome screen.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Tap the **Log In** button.

### Forgot Password

1. On the Login screen, tap **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Tap **Send Reset Link**. You will receive instructions to reset your password.

## For Trainees

### Home Screen

Your Home screen is the main hub. From here you can:
- Browse nearby trainers
- View recommended trainers
- Start searching for a trainer by specialty, location, or session type

### Finding a Trainer

1. From the Home tab, browse available trainers in your area.
2. Each trainer card shows:
   - Name and photo
   - Star rating and number of reviews
   - Specialties (e.g., HIIT, Yoga, Strength Training)
   - Session price
   - Distance from you
   - Verification badge (if verified)

3. Tap a trainer card to view their full profile.

### Trainer Detail Page

On a trainer's profile you can see:
- **Bio** — a short introduction
- **Specialties** — their training focus areas
- **Session Types** — In-Person, At Your Home, At Their Gym, Virtual
- **Pricing** — per-session cost by session type, including travel fees for in-home sessions
- **Ratings & Reviews** — from other trainees
- **Badge Level** — Rising Star, Pro, or Elite based on their track record
- **Availability** — whether they are currently accepting sessions

Tap **Book Session** to schedule training.

### Booking a Session

1. **Select Session Type** — choose from available options:
   - In-Person (at their gym)
   - At Your Home (trainer comes to you)
   - Virtual (live video session)

2. **Choose Date & Time** — pick an available slot.

3. **Review Pricing** — see the session fee, any travel fees, and the total.

4. **Confirm Booking** — review all details and tap **Confirm**.

5. **Payment** — complete payment through the secure Stripe payment screen. Your card will be charged the displayed amount.

6. After payment, you will see a confirmation screen. The trainer will be notified of your booking.

### Virtual Live Session (Need a Trainer Now)

This feature connects you with an available trainer instantly:

1. Tap **Need a Trainer Now** then select **Virtual Live Video**.

2. **Searching Phase** — the app will notify all available virtual trainers within 25 miles of your location. You will see a pulsing animation while waiting.

3. **Trainer Match** — when a trainer accepts, you will see their:
   - Photo, name, and badge level
   - Star rating and review count
   - Session price
   - Short bio

4. You then have two choices:
   - **Accept Trainer** — proceeds to the payment screen
   - **Find Another Trainer** — returns you to the search queue

5. After payment, you move into the normal session flow.

Note: If no trainer is available within a few minutes, you can cancel and try again later. You are never charged unless a session is confirmed and paid for.

### During a Session

**In-Person Sessions:**
- When both you and the trainer arrive, the trainer will start the session.
- For at-home sessions, you will receive a **4-digit Safety PIN**. Share this with your trainer to verify your identity.
- GPS confirmation may be used to verify both parties are at the agreed location.

**Session Flow:**
1. Trainer starts the session.
2. Training takes place.
3. Trainer ends the session.
4. You confirm the session is complete.

### Rating a Session

After a session is completed, you have **48 hours** to leave a rating:

1. You will see a prompt to rate your trainer.
2. Select a star rating (1 to 5 stars).
3. Optionally write a review describing your experience.
4. Tap **Submit Rating**.

You can only rate each session once.

### My Sessions

Tap the **Sessions** tab to view:
- **Upcoming** — confirmed sessions that have not happened yet
- **Active** — sessions currently in progress
- **Completed** — past sessions with status and rating info
- **Cancelled** — sessions that were cancelled

### Streaks & Achievements

RapidReps tracks your training consistency:

- **Training Streaks** — consecutive days or weeks you have trained. Keep your streak alive for bonus rewards.
- **Achievements** — earn badges for milestones like completing your first session, reaching 10 sessions, maintaining a 7-day streak, and more.
- **Leaderboard** — see how you rank against other trainees on the weekly leaderboard.

Access these from your Profile screen by tapping **Achievements** or **Leaderboard**.

### Sharing Your Progress

- **Share My Streak** — from your Profile, tap the streak counter to share your training streak on social media or with friends.
- **Share Status** — share a post-session status update celebrating your workout.

### Memberships

Memberships give you discounted session rates:

1. Go to Profile and tap **Membership**.
2. Browse available membership plans.
3. Choose a plan and complete payment.
4. Your membership status and benefits will be displayed on your profile.

### Saved Trainers

Tap the **Saved** tab to view trainers you have bookmarked. You can save a trainer from their detail page for quick access later.

### App Settings

From your **Profile** tab:
- **Sound Effects** — toggle on/off the tap sounds that play when you press buttons throughout the app.
- **Notification Preferences** — control which notifications you receive (session reminders, messages, streak alerts).
- **Edit Profile** — update your photo, fitness goals, and personal information.

## For Trainers

### Getting Verified

Before you can accept sessions, you must complete the verification process:

1. **Profile Setup** — fill in your bio, specialties, and session types you offer.
2. **Document Upload** — upload your personal training certifications.
3. **Intro Video** — record or upload a short introduction video.
4. **Location Setup** — set your training location and travel radius.
5. **Submit for Review** — once all steps are complete, submit your profile for admin review.

You can check your verification status at any time from Profile, then Verification Status.

### Onboarding Checklist

Your onboarding screen shows a checklist of required steps:
- Profile photo uploaded
- Bio written
- Certifications uploaded
- Intro video uploaded
- Pricing set
- Location configured
- Verification submitted

Complete all steps to become an active trainer on the platform.

### Setting Your Availability

1. From your **Profile** or **Home** screen, toggle your **Availability** switch.
2. When available, trainees can find you in search results and book sessions.
3. When unavailable, you will not appear in search or receive new booking requests.

### Virtual Training Toggle

1. In your profile settings, enable **Virtual Training** to accept virtual live sessions.
2. When enabled AND you are set to Available, you will receive instant notifications when a trainee requests a virtual session nearby (within 25 miles).

### Setting Your Prices

1. Go to **Edit Profile** and scroll to the Pricing section.
2. Set your rates for each session type:
   - In-Person session rate
   - At-Home session rate
   - Virtual session rate
3. Prices must be within the platform's allowed range.
4. The platform takes a 25% service fee. Your take-home is 75% of the session price.

### Managing Sessions

From your **Sessions** tab you can see:
- **Pending** — new booking requests awaiting your response
- **Upcoming** — confirmed future sessions
- **Active** — sessions currently in progress
- **Completed** — finished sessions

**Accepting or Declining Requests:**
- When a trainee books a session, you receive a notification.
- Open the session and tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
- Accepted sessions appear in your Upcoming list.

### Starting a Session

1. Navigate to the session from your Sessions tab.
2. Tap **Start Session** when you are ready to begin.
3. For at-home sessions, the trainee will share a 4-digit Safety PIN with you. Enter it to verify.
4. GPS may confirm both parties are at the correct location.

### Ending a Session

1. When training is complete, tap **End Session**.
2. The trainee will be asked to confirm the session ended.
3. Once both parties confirm, the session is marked as complete.

### Virtual Session Requests

When a trainee taps "Need a Trainer Now":

1. You will receive a push notification: *"A trainee is requesting a Virtual Live Training Session."*
2. Open the notification or go to the Virtual Requests screen.
3. You will see:
   - Trainee name
   - Session type (Virtual Live Session)
   - Start time (Now)
4. Tap **Accept** or **Reject**.
5. If you are the first trainer to accept, you are matched with the trainee. All other trainers are notified that the session has been taken.
6. The trainee will then confirm and pay. After payment, the session begins.

Note: Speed matters. The first trainer to accept wins the session.

### Earnings & Payouts

From the **Earnings** tab you can view:
- **Total Earnings** — lifetime earnings on the platform
- **Available Balance** — funds ready for payout
- **Pending** — earnings from recent sessions awaiting clearance
- **Payout History** — record of past payouts

**Requesting a Payout:**
1. Tap **Request Payout** from the Earnings screen.
2. Enter the amount you want to withdraw.
3. Confirm the request.
4. Payouts are processed by the admin team and sent to your registered payment method.

### Boosts

Boosts increase your visibility in search results:

1. Go to Profile and tap **Boosts**.
2. Choose a boost package.
3. Complete payment.
4. Your profile will be prioritized in trainee search results for the duration of the boost.

### Achievements & Badges

Trainers earn badges based on performance:

- **Rising Star** — new trainers building their reputation
- **Pro** — trainers with consistent ratings and session count
- **Elite** — top-performing trainers with exceptional ratings

Badges appear on your profile and help trainees choose you.

Additional achievements include milestones for session counts, rating thresholds, and streak maintenance.

### Editing Your Profile

From the **Profile** tab, tap **Edit Profile** to update:
- Profile photo
- Bio and introduction
- Specialties and session types
- Pricing for each session type
- Training location and travel radius
- Virtual training availability

## Messaging

### Starting a Conversation

1. Tap the **Messages** tab.
2. To start a new conversation, navigate to a trainer or trainee profile and tap **Message**.
3. Type your message and tap Send.

### Managing Conversations

- All your conversations are listed in the Messages tab.
- Each conversation shows the other person's name, last message, and timestamp.
- Tap a conversation to view the full chat history and send new messages.
- Messages are real-time. You will receive a push notification for new messages.

### Safety in Messaging

All messages are monitored for safety. You can:
- **Report** a user for inappropriate behavior
- **Block** a user to prevent further contact

## Safety & Privacy

### Safety PIN (In-Home Sessions)

For sessions at a trainee's home:
- A unique 4-digit Safety PIN is generated for each session.
- The trainee shares this PIN with the trainer at the start of the session.
- The trainer enters the PIN in the app to verify identity.

### GPS Verification

For in-person sessions, GPS may be used to confirm both parties are at the agreed location before the session starts.

### Reporting & Blocking

- **Report:** If you experience inappropriate behavior, go to the user's profile or conversation and tap **Report**. Describe the issue and submit.
- **Block:** Block a user to prevent them from contacting you or appearing in your search results.

### Privacy Policy & Terms

Access the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service from the legal section in the app (available from the signup screen and profile settings).

## Account Settings

### Editing Your Profile

Both trainers and trainees can update their profile information at any time from the Profile tab.

### Sound Effects

- Toggle button sounds on or off from your Profile screen.
- When enabled, action buttons play a short tap sound for feedback.

1. Go to your **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom.

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

**Q: How do I get matched with a virtual trainer?**
A: Tap "Need a Trainer Now" from the home screen, then "Virtual Live Video." The app will search for available trainers within 25 miles. The first trainer to accept is matched with you.

**Q: Why am I not seeing any trainers?**
A: Make sure your location is set in your profile. Trainers must be within range, verified, and have their availability toggled on.

**Q: How does pricing work?**
A: Trainers set their own session rates. The platform takes a 25% service fee. Trainees pay the full session price. Travel fees may apply for at-home sessions based on distance.

**Q: Can I cancel a session?**
A: Yes. Open the session from your Sessions tab and tap Cancel. Cancellation policies may apply depending on how close the session is to the start time.

**Q: How do payouts work for trainers?**
A: Earnings from completed sessions appear in your Available Balance. Request a payout from the Earnings tab. Payouts are processed by the admin team.

**Q: What are boosts?**
A: Boosts are paid upgrades that increase your visibility in trainee search results for a set period.

**Q: How do streaks work?**
A: Your streak counts consecutive training days or weeks. Missing a scheduled period resets your streak. Share your streak from your profile to keep motivated.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes. All payments are processed through Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. RapidReps never stores your full card details.
